The journal memory is the part of the app that remembers the owner's life. The owner talks to one agent, the Keeper, and every line she says becomes a small card. Cards are never lost and never rewritten in silence.

Other parts work from the cards. A mirror makes them searchable. Night agents tag them with people and threads. A hook hands the right cards to any agent when the owner names a person or a thread.
