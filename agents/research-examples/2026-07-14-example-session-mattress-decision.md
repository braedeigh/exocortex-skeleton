**Spark:** **Full.** Don't buy hardware for a user who doesn't exist yet — queen taxes the real things (folding routine, studio floor, budget) for a hypothetical. When a partner is real, the bigger bed is a shared decision anyway; the Full becomes the guest futon. Exchange policy is same-size-only, so pick for the life being lived.

**Spark:** **No.** Nothing mechanical to fail in wool+latex; realistic damage (stains/moisture) is what plans deny and what the protector + tatami already cover. Checkout protection plans are margin, not coverage.
